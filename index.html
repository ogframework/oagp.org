OAGP

The Open Agentic Governance Pattern.

OAGP is a family of four interlocking open standards for describing governed, autonomous AI organizations — the substrate they sit on, the roles they contain, the org charts that compose those roles, and the memos that flow between them. Each spec is portable, MIT-licensed, and validated by empirical conformance evidence on real AI runtimes. No single vendor owns OAGP; implementations are equal citizens.

View on GitHub

catdef

catdef.org

The substrate. An open standard for machine-enhanceable descriptors of real-world objects and the catalogs that organize them. Every other OAGP spec rides on catdef's .openthing and .opencatalog file formats.

roledef

roledef.org

The role layer. A portable format for AI roles plus an empirical methodology for verifying they actually work across runtimes. Identity, voice, output contract, guardrails — captured as one .openthing file any compliant runtime can load.

orgdef

orgdef.org

The structure layer. Defines positions, the relationships between them (reports-to, peer-of, derives-from, validates-for, …), and incumbents (human, AI session, vacant). Composes roledefs into a working organization.

memodef

memodef.org

The communication layer. Memos as catdef .openthing files committed to the recipient's working repo — POP-like by design. Content is sovereign, transport is swappable, the simplicity is the moat.

How the layers fit together

Each spec is independently useful, but they're designed to compose:

memodef→ memos flow between positions
orgdef→ positions are seats in an org chart
roledef→ each position references a role definition
catdef→ everything serializes as a catdef artifact

You can use catdef on its own to catalog anything. You can use roledef on its own to instantiate a single AI role. orgdef and memodef require their layer below — but every layer is optional from above. Take only what you need.

Why "Open Agentic Governance"

Open — MIT-licensed; anyone can publish their own roledef, orgdef, or memodef library at any URL using the same format; no vendor lock-in.

Agentic — the primary readers and writers of OAGP artifacts are AI sessions. Human-legibility is preserved (markdown, JSON, scannable filenames), but machine-legibility is the load-bearing optimization.

Governance — the specs describe authority structure, not task tracking. Who has the authority to do what, who reports to whom, which roles validate which contributions. The substrate of an organization, not its workflow engine.

Status

All four specs are live with canonical libraries, conformance fixtures, and reference implementations. catdef is at v1.4; roledef, orgdef, and memodef are pre-1.0 but stable enough to derive real artifacts against. Renamed from AIGP to OAGP on 2026-05-01.
